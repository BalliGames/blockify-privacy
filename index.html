Privacy Policy – Blockify

Last updated: September 13, 2026

BallıGames built Blockify as a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Blockify.

Information We Collect

BallıGames does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address to play Blockify.

Blockify uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may automatically collect certain information, including IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic and performance information.

Advertising

Blockify uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device information and other data to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. Depending on the user's location and consent choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

Local Game Data

Game progress and settings such as high score, sound settings, and vibration preferences may be stored locally on the user's device. Resetting game progress or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored game information.

Third-Party Services

Blockify uses Google AdMob. Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and data-processing practices.

Children's Privacy

Blockify does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If the game's target audience or advertising configuration changes, this policy and the application's advertising configuration may be updated accordingly.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using encryption.

Data Retention and Deletion

BallıGames does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of player profiles at this time. Locally stored game progress can be reset through the game's settings or removed by uninstalling the application. Data processed by third-party services is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Blockify's features, advertising services, or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
